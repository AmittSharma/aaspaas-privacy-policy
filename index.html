Privacy Policy for Aaspaas Grocery List Aaspaas Grocery List is a local grocery list app. The app allows users to create, save, duplicate, export, and share grocery lists. Information stored by the app: - User name - Mobile number - Grocery lists - Item quantities - Optional item notes or brand preferences This information is stored locally on the user’s device. We do not operate a backend server for this version of the app, and we do not collect, sell, or share user data with our own servers. Users may choose to export a grocery list as a PDF and share it using apps installed on their device, such as WhatsApp, email, or other sharing apps. This sharing action is controlled by the user. The app does not include ads, analytics, payments, account login, or online ordering. If you have privacy questions, contact us at: aaspaas.support@gmail.com
